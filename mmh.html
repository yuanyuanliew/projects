品牌介紹

一個從電視台走出來、又回到內容矩陣的理財 IP。

「全家私房錢 Money Money Home」自馬來西亞 Astro 電視台起家，曾製播兩季共 26 集半小時理財節目，是少數在新馬市場長期經營「全家一起談錢」定位的中文內容品牌。節目下線後，品牌延續原班底經營內容矩陣，累積 8 萬 Facebook 粉絲與 2 萬 YouTube 訂閱，並持續與政府機構、上市公司、發展商合作策劃線下活動與內容項目。

隨著內容消費習慣轉移，全家私房錢 2.0 以同一班底、全新面貌重新出發，深耕 YouTube 為主頻道，同步經營 Facebook 與 Instagram，將原本的電視棚內敘事能力，轉化為短影音時代的週更內容與 Shorts／Reels。

品牌官方陳述 — Money Money Home

我們獨到地融合知識性與娛樂性內容，讓理財知識不論新手或專家都能輕鬆理解、人人可及。

Brand Introduction

A finance IP that started on TV and returned to the content matrix.

Money Money Home began on Malaysia's Astro TV, producing two seasons and 26 half-hour finance episodes — one of the few Chinese-language brands in the Malaysia-Singapore market built around "the whole family talking about money." After the show ended, the same core team kept the brand alive across a content matrix, growing to 80,000 Facebook fans and 20,000 YouTube subscribers, while continuing to co-organize offline events and content projects with government bodies, listed companies and property developers.

As content consumption habits shift, Money Money Home 2.0 relaunches with the same team and a fresh look, focusing on YouTube as its main channel while running Facebook and Instagram in parallel — turning its original TV-studio storytelling into weekly content and Shorts／Reels for the short-video era.
