一　公司背景与商业模式 I　Company Background & Business Model

内容建立信任，交易在内容之外发生Content builds trust; the transaction happens outside it

The Standard Media（TSM）是「全家私房钱 2.0」内容生态系下的商业化实体。运作的逻辑其实很简单：用真实人物与产业故事赢得大众信任，内容里完全不卖东西、不置入任何产品或服务。等一段内容走到尾声，系统会从高意向的观众里抛出精准的 Hook，真正的成交与商业化，交给业务团队与合作伙伴在内容之外完成。 The Standard Media (TSM) is the commercial entity behind the MoneyMoneyHome 2.0 content ecosystem. The logic behind it is fairly simple: real people and real industry stories earn public trust, and the content itself never pitches a product or service. Once a piece of content runs its course, a precise hook goes out to whichever viewers show high intent, and the business team and partners take it from there, closing the actual deal entirely outside the content.

第一个内容企划《Coffee & Capital》，锁定的是厂房与工业地产（大马规模最大、却也最不透明的一类资产）。内容累积下来的信任，最终转化成两条收入线：地产转介佣金，以及内容业配（详见第五章）。 The first project, Coffee & Capital, focuses on factories and industrial real estate, Malaysia's largest asset class and also its least transparent. The trust that content builds up gets converted into two revenue engines: real-estate referral commission and content sponsorship (see Chapter V).

说明　本章商业模式与后续财务数字，整理自《The Standard 全家私房钱 2.0 企划书》与《全家私房钱 Money Money Home 媒体招商手册》，作为本股权建议书的事实基础。 Note　The business model and financial figures in this document are drawn from The Standard MoneyMoneyHome 2.0 Proposal and the MoneyMoneyHome Media Sales Kit, forming the factual basis for this equity proposal.

3.1　股权即时全额归属（不设 Vesting）3.1　Equity Vests Immediately in Full (No Vesting Schedule)

建议双方股权在公司成立、股东协议签署时即 100% 归属，不设分期兑现（Vesting）机制或 Cliff。理由是：Vesting 通常用来保障「用未来的承诺换股权」这种情况——公司担心的是创办人才做了两个月就走人，却拿走大部分股份。但这套逻辑不完全适用于这次的架构：Ms Coffy Yap 的 RM 1,000,000 是一次性到位的资本，理所当然全额即时归属；刘元元的核心筹码——全家私房钱 IP／品牌资产（两季 26 集节目、80,000+ FB 粉丝、20,000+ YouTube 订阅、364 万历史观看、已验证的信任资产）——同样是她过去多年已经建立、即时带进 TSM 的既有资产，不是靠在 TSM 未来慢慢累积出来的承诺。既然两边的核心筹码都是「即时到位」，股权归属的时间点也应该一致：都不设 Vesting。至于刘元元往后持续投入的全职劳动，则透过第八章的 CEO 薪酬机制单独、按月支付，不需要再靠股权分期兑现来做「防止中途离开」的保险。 Both parties' equity is proposed to vest 100% immediately at incorporation / signing of the shareholders' agreement, with no vesting schedule or cliff. Vesting typically exists to protect against a founder trading a future promise for equity — the company's worry is someone leaving after two months while walking away with most of the stock. That logic doesn't map cleanly onto this structure: Ms Coffy Yap's RM 1,000,000 is a one-time capital injection, so it's straightforward for it to vest in full immediately; Yuan Yuan's core chip — the MoneyMoneyHome IP/brand asset (2 seasons/26 episodes, 80,000+ FB fans, 20,000+ YouTube subs, 3.64M lifetime views, a proven trust asset) — is likewise an existing asset she built over years and brings to TSM on day one, not a promise to be earned gradually on TSM's clock. Since both sides' core chips are delivered upfront, the vesting timing should match: neither vests over time. Yuan Yuan's ongoing full-time labor going forward is instead compensated directly and separately through the CEO salary mechanism in Chapter VIII, paid monthly — it doesn't need equity vesting to serve as an "early-departure" insurance policy.

若刘元元离职　股权不受影响，公司另聘新 CEO 接手日常营运即可——这是与「即时全额归属」逻辑一致的立场，而非疏忽：股权既然不是靠她持续在职才慢慢兑现，那么反过来，离职也不该触发任何形式的股权回购或没收，否则等于换个名目重新引入 Vesting（用「Leaver 条款」取代「兑现时间表」，本质上仍是把股权跟在职与否绑在一起）。CEO 是一个可替换的营运角色，全家私房钱这项 IP／品牌资产才是她换取 60% 股权的对价，而这项资产已在签署时即时、完整地带进 TSM，不因日后由谁担任 CEO 而改变归属。 If Yuan Yuan leaves　equity is unaffected; the company simply hires a new CEO to run day-to-day operations. This follows directly from — not as an exception to — the "vests immediately in full" logic: if equity isn't earned gradually by staying, then leaving shouldn't trigger any buyback or forfeiture either, since doing so would just reintroduce vesting under another name (a leaver clause substitutes a repurchase trigger for a vesting schedule, but both still tie equity to continued tenure). CEO is a replaceable operating role; the MoneyMoneyHome IP/brand asset is what she traded for her 60%, and that asset is delivered to TSM in full at signing, regardless of who holds the CEO title afterward.
  • 若未来引入外部融资、员工期权池（ESOP）或新股东，双方股权原则上按比例同步稀释，除非另行约定优先认购权（Pre-emptive Right）或反稀释条款。 Future outside funding, an ESOP pool or new shareholders would dilute both parties proportionally, absent a separately agreed pre-emptive right or anti-dilution clause.

八　创办人薪酬与盈利分配机制 VIII　Founder Compensation & Profit Distribution

薪酬付出的是劳力，分润付出的是股权Salary pays for labor; profit share pays for equity

刘元元全职出任 CEO，理论上可以、也建议同时透过两条不同性质的通道从 TSM 取得报酬：作为员工领取市场水平的固定月薪，以及作为 60% 股东按比例领取盈利分红。两者性质不同、不互相替代，也不构成「重复拿钱」——Ms Coffy Yap 同样以 40% 股权分享公司利润，却不需要投入全职人力；刘元元的薪酬正是对这部分「Coffy 不需要做、但元元必须做」的劳力与机会成本的补偿。 As full-time CEO, Yuan Yuan can — and should — draw pay from TSM through two distinct channels: a market-rate fixed monthly salary as an employee, and a profit distribution as a 60% shareholder. The two aren't the same thing, and one doesn't substitute for the other, nor is it "double-dipping": Ms Coffy Yap also shares in company profit through her 40% stake without contributing full-time labor. Yuan Yuan's salary is precisely the compensation for the labor and opportunity cost Coffy doesn't have to put in.

8.3　盈利分配的处理顺序8.3　Profit Distribution Waterfall

可分配利润不是把净利直接对半分掉，而是依序走完下图的处理顺序——本金归还排在两位股东分红之前，是整套机制最核心的一点。 Distributable profit isn't split straight down the line — it runs through the sequence below in order. Capital return sits ahead of both shareholders' dividend — the single most important point in this mechanism.

  1. 总收入　地产分润（引擎一）＋业配净收入（引擎二），见第五章Revenue　Real-estate commission (Engine One) + net sponsorship income (Engine Two), per Chapter V
  2. 减：营运成本　含 CEO 薪酬，见 8.1／8.2Less operating costs　incl. CEO salary, per 8.1/8.2
  3. 减：内容团队激励奖金池　税前利润的 8%，税前可扣，不论由谁主持都存在，详见 8.5Less content talent incentive pool　8% of pre-tax profit, tax-deductible, exists regardless of who hosts; see 8.5
  4. 减：企业所得税　大马 SME 阶梯税率（15% / 17% / 24%，以会计师确认为准）Less corporate tax　Malaysia's tiered SME rate (15%/17%/24%, subject to accountant confirmation)
  5. 减：预留储备金　Y1–Y2 约 25%，Y3 后视现金状况调降Less retained reserve　~25% in Y1–Y2, scaling down from Y3
  6. = 可分配利润= Distributable profit
  7. 减：Ms Coffy Yap 本金归还　优先于两位股东分红，年度上限为可分配利润的 40%，详见 8.6Less Ms Coffy Yap's capital return　ahead of both shareholders' dividends, capped at 40% of distributable profit per year; see 8.6
  8. = 两位股东分红　剩余部分才按 60%（刘元元）／ 40%（Ms Coffy Yap）分配= Shareholder dividend　Only the remainder splits 60% (Yuan Yuan) / 40% (Ms Coffy Yap)
关于顺序的补充　奖金池与本金归还性质不同：奖金池是税前可扣税费用，影响利润表本身；本金归还只影响可分配利润如何分给两位股东，不影响税项计算，但同样排在分红之前。两者都不改变两位股东 60/40 的分配比例本身。 A note on the order　The pool and capital return work differently: the pool is a pre-tax deductible expense that affects the P&L itself, while capital return only affects how distributable profit is split — it doesn't touch the tax calculation, but it still sits ahead of the dividend. Neither changes the underlying 60/40 ratio.

8.6　Ms Coffy Yap 启动资金的分阶段归还8.6　Phased Return of Ms Coffy Yap's Startup Capital

RM 1,000,000 已是第六章 Y1 核心预算，无法一次性退还；厂房交易周期长达 3–6 个月（见第十章风险二），因此本金归还直接挂在 8.3 的分配顺序上，随实收现金自然发生，不另立固定还款时间表。 RM 1,000,000 is already Chapter VI's core Y1 budget and can't be repaid in one lump sum; factory deals take 3–6 months to close (Chapter X, Risk 2), so capital return is hung directly off 8.3's distribution order — happening naturally as cash is collected, with no separate fixed repayment calendar.

机制：本金归还优先于两位股东分润，但每年封顶为可分配利润的 40%Mechanism: capital return takes priority over both shareholders' dividends, capped at 40% of distributable profit per year

  1. 当年本金归还 ＝「剩余未还本金」与「可分配利润的 40%」两者中较小者——上限确保任何一年两人都不会分润归零This year's capital return = the smaller of the remaining unrecovered principal and 40% of distributable profit — the cap ensures neither shareholder's dividend ever drops to zero
  2. 划出本金归还后，剩余才按 60%（刘元元）／ 40%（Ms Coffy Yap）分红——本金未还清期间，Ms Coffy Yap 实收（本金＋分红）会高于单纯 40%，刘元元实收会低于单纯 60%，这正是「优先于分润」的实际效果After capital return is set aside, the remainder splits 60% (Yuan Yuan) / 40% (Ms Coffy Yap) as ordinary dividend — while principal is outstanding, Ms Coffy Yap's total (capital + dividend) runs above a plain 40% share, Yuan Yuan's below a plain 60% share — this is the real effect of "ahead of both shareholders' dividends"
  3. 哪一年剩余本金低于 40% 上限，当年归还金额改按剩余本金计算；还清后不再有这一步，往后每年全部恢复正常 60/40In whichever year the remaining principal falls below the 40% cap, that year's return is set at the remaining balance instead; once fully repaid, this step drops out and every later year reverts to a plain 60/40
  4. 可分配利润为零或负的年份不归还，余额不计息、原数结转下一年In a zero-or-negative-profit year, nothing is returned; the balance carries forward at no interest

账面净利仅 RM 3.7 万–25 万，理论上没亏钱，但「打平」不等于「有现金」——已签约的佣金可能还卡在返佣流程里。Net profit on paper is only RM 37,000–250,000 — technically not a loss, but "breakeven" isn't "cash in hand": signed commissions may still be moving through the remittance pipeline.

方案　Fix　该年度暂停利润分配、全数留存（8.3 的 25% 储备金比例上调至 50%+，直到 Y2 资金确认到位）；Y2 预算按季核定释放，而非年初一次拨足；同样优先冲刺业配。Pause profit distribution and retain all distributable profit for the year (raise the 8.3 reserve ratio to 50%+ until Y2 funding is confirmed); release the Y2 budget quarterly against confirmed cash rather than committing it upfront; again prioritize sponsorship sales.

情况三Scenario 3Y1 明显超过 9–10 间Comfortably over 9–10 deals

现金盈余较厚，足以覆盖 Y2 大部分甚至全部预算——原始「公司自己造血」假设真正成立的情况。The cash surplus is thick enough to plausibly cover most or all of Y2's budget — the one scenario where the original "self-funds" assumption actually holds.

方案　Fix　仍先确认年结时有多少是「真正到账」的现金，预留一部分跨年缓冲，其余才按 8.3／8.6 处理。Still confirm at year-end how much has actually landed in the bank, hold back a cross-year buffer, and only then run the rest through 8.3/8.6.

建议　不论落在哪种情况，Y1 Q3（约 9 月）按「实际到账现金」盘点，作为是否启动应急资金、下修 Y2 预算的依据（第四章季度议程）。应急资金若不计入股权资本，也可改为短期股东借款——不稀释、不改变股权与分润结构，仅约定还本条件。 Recommendation　Whichever scenario plays out, check actual cash received at Y1 Q3 (≈September) as the trigger for drawing the contingency facility or scaling back Y2 (Chapter IV's quarterly agenda). If preferred, the facility can be structured as a short-term shareholder loan instead — no dilution, no change to equity or profit split, just agreed repayment terms.

十　关键风险提示与建议 X　Key Risks & Recommendations

签署前应厘清的四项风险Four risks worth clearing up before signing

以下四项建议在双方签署正式股东协议前一并厘清，属于对 Ms Coffy Yap 之投资权益的必要保障事项：These four items are worth resolving before the shareholders' agreement is signed, as necessary protection for Ms Coffy Yap's investment:

01营收集中风险Revenue Concentration

Y3 预测营收中约 90.8% 来自单一资产类别（厂房／工业地产）分润，其他内容线目前贡献极小。≈90.8% of Y3 forecast revenue comes from one asset class (factory/industrial real estate); other content lines currently contribute very little.

建议　Fix　将其他内容线的商业化验证列为 Y2 重点目标之一。Make monetizing the other content lines a Y2 priority.

02现金周期错配Cash-Cycle Mismatch

内容与团队成本按月前置支出，厂房交易周期长达 3–6 个月，Y1 恐出现现金缺口。Content and team costs are paid monthly up front, while factory deals take 3–6 months to close, so a Y1 cash gap is possible.

建议　Fix　预留营运周转金，或将启动资金分阶段拨付、与里程碑挂钩；同样的现金周期，也是启动资金要分阶段归还给 Ms Coffy Yap（第八章 8.6），以及 Y1→Y2 需要一笔应急资金与情境应对方案（第八章 8.7）的原因。Hold a working-capital buffer, or release funds in stages tied to milestones; the same cash cycle is why the startup capital is returned to Ms Coffy Yap in phases (Chapter VIII, 8.6), and why the Y1→Y2 transition needs a contingency facility and scenario plans (Chapter VIII, 8.7).

03单一供应商依赖Single-Supplier Dependency

Y1 几乎 100% 仰赖 NCT 单一货源；储备虽充足，仍属单点风险。Y1 relies almost entirely on NCT as the sole supply source; reserves are ample but it remains a single point of risk.

建议　Fix　Co-broke 伙伴网络开发从 Y2 提前至 Y1 同步启动。Start building the co-broke network in Y1, not Y2.

04固定成本、变动营收Fixed Cost, Variable Revenue

Worst 情境下固定成本几乎吃掉全部营收增长空间，下行保护有限。Under Worst, fixed costs consume nearly all revenue upside, leaving little downside protection.

建议　Fix　设定阶段性 KPI 作为资金分批到位与治理复核的依据。Set staged KPIs to gate further fund releases and governance review.
